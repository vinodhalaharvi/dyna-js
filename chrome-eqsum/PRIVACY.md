# Equation Summary privacy policy

Equation Summary does not collect, store or transmit any personal or user data.

- The PDF you choose is downloaded by your browser and processed entirely on your device. Its contents are never sent to any server.
- The extension uses no analytics, no tracking, no cookies and no remote code.
- Host access is requested only for the site whose PDF you ask to summarise, and only to download that PDF.
- Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, because nothing is collected.

Questions: https://github.com/vinodhalaharvi/dyna-js/issues
